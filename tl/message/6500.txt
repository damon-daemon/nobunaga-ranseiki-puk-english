16th head of the Date. 2nd son of Harumune. Often fought the Soma. A year after passing the headship to Masamune, seized by Nihonmatsu Yoshitsugu and killed in Date gunfire during the rescue.
@6502
17th head of the Date. Eldest son of Terumune. Subdued neighbours in no time and at 24 ruled Oshu, feared as the "One-Eyed Dragon." Survived the Toyotomi and Tokugawa regimes by cunning.
@6504
3rd son of Tanemune. Meant to be adopted into the Echigo Uesugi, but his brother Harumune blocked it, causing the Tenbun War. Sided with Tanemune in the war, then served Harumune afterward.
@6506
Son of Sanemoto. Aided his lord Masamune in "martial" matters as the Date's boldest general, judged "of heroic and great planning." In old age told Oshu war tales to the 3rd Tokugawa Shogun Iemitsu.
@6508
3rd son of Date Harumune. Wed a daughter of Rusu Akimune and took over the Rusu. Served brother Terumune and nephew Masamune against the Soma and Mogami. Given the Date name after Korea.
@6510
Date vassal. Became Masamune's tutor at 19 and a wise general aiding him in "wisdom." Urged Masamune to join Hideyoshi's Odawara campaign, helping the Date survive.
@6512
Date vassal. Son of Kagetsuna. Won fame in the Osaka summer campaign, killing Goto Matabei and others; Tokugawa Ieyasu called him a "demon." Known for beauty; tale says Kobayakawa Hideaki chased him.
@6514
Date vassal. When Tanemune's 5th son Moroshige was adopted into the Kokubun, went there to govern. Later a council member. Though old, served as rearguard at Hitotoribashi and fell.
@6516
Date vassal. Masamune's confidant. In the Korean campaign was given Hideyoshi's favourite Ko no Mae, angered Masamune and deserted. Later returned and handled state affairs as elder.
@6518
Date vassal. After Nakano Munetoki's fall, trusted by Terumune and made senior elder. Handled government and corresponded with Oda Nobunaga. After Terumune's death followed him in death at his grave.
@6520
Date vassal. In the Tenbun War served his father-in-law Tanemune. Angry that the peace terms razed his castle Kakeda, rebelled but was captured and executed.
@6522
Date vassal. In the Tenbun War served Tanemune, rescuing him from confinement at Nishiyama Castle. Attended Tanemune after the war and followed him in death.
@6524
Date vassal. Son of Munetomo. In the Tenbun War served Tanemune with his father. Led the van against Nakano Munetoki's rebellion and fell at the siege of Komatsu Castle. Skilled in letters and arms.
@6526
Date vassal. Son of Chikamune. Led the van against the Mogami, joined the campaign against Ouchi Sadatsuna, and was rated a brave and wise counsellor. In old age attended Masamune, offering advice.
@6528

@6530

@6532
Date vassal. Served Terumune and Masamune, winning merit in many battles. At Suriagehara formed the Date main force with Date Shigezane. Also joined Hideyoshi's Korean campaign.
@6534
Date vassal. Staff officer to Harumune in the Tenbun War, then the strongest in the house. Rebelled under Terumune but lost and fled; wandered and starved to death.
@6536
Date vassal. 2nd son of Nakano Munetoki. Oshu Deputy Constable who ran Date government with his father. Rebelled under Terumune, lost and fled into Soma land.
@6538
Date vassal. Given command of troops at 18 and won merit everywhere, a bold warrior. Died of illness on Hideyoshi's Korean campaign. Harada Kai of the "Date Disturbance" was Munetoki's grandson.
@6540
Date vassal. A member of the Keicho Embassy to Europe, visited Spain and Rome. Returned with little to show and died in despair two years later.
@6542
Date vassal. Attended Masamune at 14. Outstanding in wit and valour, left in charge when Masamune was away. Dispossessed for arrogance and died of illness while wandering.
@6544

@6546
17th head of the Satake. Eldest son of Yoshiatsu. Subdued the Shirakawa Yuki of southern Mutsu and fought the Oda for Hitachi supremacy. Refused Kanto Kanrei Uesugi Norimasa's plea for shelter.
@6548
18th head of the Satake. Eldest son of Yoshiaki. Carried out his father's will and unified Hitachi. Made enemies of the Hojo and Date; his baton-waving at the front won him the fear name "Demon Yoshishige."
@6550
19th head of the Satake. Eldest son of Yoshishige. Known as a fierce general like his father. Resolved to join the Western Army at Sekigahara; Tokugawa Ieyasu called him "a rare honest man of this age."
@6552

@6554
Satake kin. 2nd son of Higashi house's Yoshikata. Commanded rule of southern Mutsu and was active in government, war and diplomacy. Befriended Hideyoshi through Ishida Mitsunari.
@6556
Satake kin. 2nd son of Kita house's Yoshinobu. Inherited when his brother Yoshizumi was killed. Served young head Yoshiaki, with Higashi's Yoshikata and Minami's Yoshisato running Satake affairs in turn.

@6560

@6564

@6566

@6568

@6570

@6572
Satake vassal. Close aide to Yoshishige and Yoshinobu, key in diplomacy. At the fief reallocation served as one of Yoshinobu's three magistrates with Wada Akitame and Hitomi Fujimichi.
@6574

Satake vassal. The Funao descend from Takasuke, brother of Iwaki Yoshitaka. Served the Satake under Yoshiaki, Yoshishige and Yoshinobu, fighting in the van in Mutsu.
@6578
Satake vassal. Briefly served the Shirakawa Yuki, then served Yoshiaki, Yoshishige and Yoshinobu in domestic affairs and diplomacy. Stayed home during Hideyoshi's Korean campaign and ran the domain.

@6584
Hitachi gentry. Lord of Mito Castle. Son of Tadamichi. Sickly, he soon gave the headship to his son Shigemichi and died of illness shut in Takekuma Castle outside Mito. Copies of his vassal commissions survive.
@6586
Hitachi gentry. Lord of Mito Castle. Son of Michimasa. Allied with the Uesugi against the Hojo. After Odawara refused to give up Mito Castle, was driven out by Satake Yoshinobu and fled to Yuki land.
@6588

@6590
Hitachi gentry. Lord of Makabe Castle. Followed the Satake. Showed valour swinging a ten-foot wooden staff instead of blade or spear, feared as "Demon Makabe." Followed the Satake to Akita.
@6592
Edo vassal. After the Hojo fell, when his house refused to yield Mito Castle and was attacked by Satake Yoshinobu, he fought the Satake at Katsukura Castle and fell.
@6594

@6598
Hitachi gentry. Lord of Fuchu Castle. Son of Sadakuni. Skipped Hideyoshi's Odawara campaign, was attacked by Satake Yoshinobu and killed himself. His kin were also lured to Ota Castle and murdered.
@6600
14th head of the Oda. Son of Horikoshi Kubo Ashikaga Masatomo. Adopted by Oda Shigeharu. Fought the Edo and others, backed Koga Kubo Ashikaga at Kawagoe, and campaigned variously.

@6626
Koga Ashikaga vassal. Lord of Sekiyado Castle. Son of Harusuke. Followed Uesugi Kenshin into the Kanto, angering Hojo Ujimasa. Pardoned and restored, but driven out for dealing with Takeda Shingen.
@6628

@6630
16th head of the Yuki. Son of Masatomo. Strengthened control with the "Yuki Shinhatto" code. Allied with Hojo Ujiyasu, expanding in north Kanto, and kept fighting the Satake, Utsunomiya and Oda.
@6632
17th head of the Yuki. 2nd son of Oyama Takatomo. Adopted by his uncle Masakatsu. Shifted between the Hojo and Uesugi. Later adopted Tokugawa Ieyasu's 2nd son Hideyasu.
@6634
Shimotsuke gentry. 2nd son of Yuki Masatomo. Adopted by Oyama Masanaga. Restored Oyama power with the backing of his brother Yuki Masakatsu. Allied with the Hojo as well and ruled his domain stably.
@6636
Shimotsuke gentry. Eldest son of Takatomo. With brother Yuki Haretomo, allied with the Uesugi and Hojo to keep the Oyama alive, but sided with the Hojo at Odawara and lost his lands.
@6638
Yuki vassal. The Yamakawa descend from Shigemitsu, son of Yuki founder Tomomitsu. With the Taga, Mizunoya and Iwakami called the "Four Guardians of the Yuki", with strong influence on the main house.
@6640
Yuki vassal. Lord of Shimozuma Castle. Son of Masatsune. Joined Hideyoshi's Odawara campaign, lands confirmed. Joined the West at Sekigahara, was dispossessed and died wandering in Hikone, Omi.
@6642
Yuki vassal. Active in diplomacy, as envoy to the Satake and Utsunomiya. Arranged Tokugawa Ieyasu's 2nd son Hideyasu's adoption into the Yuki and was praised by Hideyoshi.
@6644
Yuki vassal. Lord of Shimodate Castle. One of the Four Guardians of the Yuki. Mostly marched toward Shimotsuke, fighting the Utsunomiya. Joined Hideyoshi's Odawara campaign, lands confirmed.
@6646
Yuki vassal. Born a Miura. Allowed by lord Haretomo to take the Iwakami name of one of the Four Guardians of the Yuki. Active in diplomacy, corresponding with Satake Yoshishige and Tamura Kiyoaki.
@6648
Oyumi Palace. Son of Koga Kubo Masauji. Fell out with his father and others, wandered, then entered Oyumi Castle with the Mariya Takeda's backing. Allied with the Satomi against the Hojo at Kounodai and fell.
@6650
Lord of Kitsuregawa Castle, Shimotsuke. Son of Oyumi Palace Yoshiaki. Wandered after his father fell in the First Battle of Kounodai. Later served Hideyoshi. His daughter Shimako became Hideyoshi's concubine.
@6652
Lord of Kitsuregawa Castle, Shimotsuke. 2nd son of Yoriuji. His brother Kunitomo died on Hideyoshi's Korean campaign, so Yoriuji wed his widow (Ashikaga Yoshiuji's daughter) and took his lands.
@6654
Oyumi Ashikaga vassal. Served as intermediary for lord Yoshiaki. In the First Battle of Kounodai, a guard around Yoshiaki. After Yoshiaki fell, took young Yorizumi to the Satomi.
@6656
Oyumi Ashikaga vassal. Styled Hayato no suke. In the First Battle of Kounodai he held Sagamidai to the north, was gravely wounded in a Hojo surprise attack and died after retreating to Kounodai.
@6658
Oyumi Ashikaga vassal. The Henmi were the main Kai Genji line; followed Kamakura Kubo Mochiuji in the Eikyo War and killed himself. Fought as a guard in the First Battle of Kounodai and fell.
@6660
